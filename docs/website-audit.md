## Current SEO structure
- Titles are unique and mostly well-formed (`<Service> Agency/Company in Coimbatore | Phoenixx IT`); homepage title: *Phoenixx IT — IT & Digital Solutions, Vadavalli, Coimbatore*.
- Meta descriptions exist on all pages (3 slightly over 160 chars).
- Canonicals are present and self-referencing on every page ✔.
- Homepage has one H1; FAQ page has valid `FAQPage` JSON-LD ✔.

## Immediate technical SEO concerns (by severity)
1. **HIGH – No H1 on any of the 10 inner pages.** Service pages open with an `<h2>` (e.g. "Workflow Automation"). Fix: promote to a descriptive H1 ("Business Automation Services in Coimbatore").
2. **HIGH – Homepage Organization JSON-LD is invalid JSON** (`"contactPoint"` is outside the object), so Google ignores the whole block. Fix: valid Organization + WebSite + ProfessionalService blocks (generated by the agent from the verified NAP).
3. **HIGH – Thin service pages** (~260–330 words including navigation). They don't explain process, deliverables or FAQs well enough to compete for commercial queries.
4. **HIGH – Slow server response** on some pages (crawler measured 1.5–4 s to first byte). Check hosting/caching; verify with PageSpeed Insights.
5. **MEDIUM – Visible encoding errors** on service pages: dashes render as `â€”` (double-encoded UTF-8).
6. **MEDIUM – No LocalBusiness/ProfessionalService schema** despite a physical Vadavalli office.
7. **LOW – Service pages don't link to each other** (only homepage anchors) → weak topical linking.
8. **LOW – Unverifiable claims** ("60% manual workload reduction", "99% reliability") – keep only if they can be substantiated.
9. **LOW – `og:image` is a relative URL** (`images/logo.png`) → broken social previews; duplicate `google-site-verification` meta; no Service schema on service pages; stock images hot-linked from Unsplash.
